About Windy

I’m a writer, facilitator, and advocate. I write fiction and memoir, create spaces where writers can take chances, and advocate for people whose voices can get lost in complicated systems.

Community & Workshops

I believe a safe space can help writers flourish as they work their craft. In the group we have grown, ideas and encouragement are freely shared in a judgment-free environment where criticism has no place to fester or settle.

I ask and I listen. In our first few groups, three writers expressed an interest in memoir, prompting me to take a deep dive of my own. Having already written a memoir made the process easier, yet challenging as I developed a four-group arc designed for creative nonfiction as well as plain ole fiction.

What surprised me was where one of those groups took us. By narrowing a story down to one moment and working through seven steps to bring it into detail, something clicked. The interest it sparked led us somewhere new.

What Baraboo Writers Blast provides are literary and creative prompts, sometimes so silly, your side hurts. The laughter is infectious. Quick-Fires and unexpected challenges find their way in between the deeper work, giving us room to loosen up, experiment, and see where a few words might take us.

Everyone is a writer. Whether the words are written down or spoken aloud, they count. There is no right way to participate, and no one has to prove they belong in the room.

Upcoming Groups & Workshops

Ink & Imagination

September 17, 2026 — The Time Capsule Key
What story can an ordinary object hold? Vintage objects become the starting point as we use sensory details, imagination, and a little mystery to uncover the stories hiding behind them.

October 15, 2026 — The Borrowed Perspective
Step outside yourself and into someone else's point of view. We'll experiment with voice, personality, and perspective to discover how differently two characters can experience the very same moment.

November 19, 2026 — The Elephant in the Room
Two characters. One confined space. And something neither one wants to talk about. We'll play with dialogue, tension, humor, and everything happening between the lines when the biggest thing in the room remains unsaid.

December 17, 2026 — The Snapshot Story
Can you tell an entire story in exactly 100 words? We'll take on the challenge of flash fiction, where every word earns its place and a single paragraph can hold an entire moment.

Ink & Imagination is a library-sponsored workshop series at Carnegie-Schadde Memorial Public Library.

Baraboo Writers Blast

September 23, 2026 — Chapter Book Development: Step 3 — The Breaking Point (The Crisis)
Every story reaches a point where something has to give. We'll explore the crisis—the moment when pressure peaks, choices matter, and a chapter or story can no longer continue in quite the same direction.

October 21, 2026 — Chapter Book Development: Step 4 — The Internal Shift (The New Path)
What changes after the breaking point? We'll explore the internal shift that follows a crisis and how that change can move a character—and the story—onto a new path.

Both Chapter Book Development groups meet in the W.R. and Floy A. Sauey Foundation Community Meeting Room at Carnegie-Schadde Memorial Public Library.

Writing & Books

The Sleeping State

What if the fear you imagine isn't a memory inside you, but your insides bursting from a place only you can't explain?

The Sleeping State began with a fearful moment drawn from a dream. With each moment since, my world grew with every word my ink drew down, long before my fingers ever touched the keys.

Dread intimidates Gary's shadow with a lurking presence only he can place but never explain. Demanding another life while Gary lives what any human is supposed to yearn for.

His young son knew building skyscrapers were his father's direction. A peak not met yet.

Truth not spoken.

The story does not end here. It spreads into a world not my own, built by a mind wanting more.

Published · Trilogy continues with The Sleeping Planet and The Perfect Sleep — both in progress

20 Memories I Could Live Without

Spanning more than five decades, 20 Memories I Could Live Without reaches from childhood violence and family dysfunction to homelessness, generational mental illness, multiple deaths, and losses that change your very soul. Not all memories feel like my own, and maybe they aren't. Ordinary they may seem, but my normal is mine to bear.
