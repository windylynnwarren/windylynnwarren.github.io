About Windy

I am a writer, author, facilitator, and community advocate. My work crosses creative writing, community engagement, lived experience, and systems-level thinking.

Whether I am writing a story, building a space for other writers, or asking difficult questions about the systems people depend on, I am interested in what happens when people speak—and what changes when someone listens.
